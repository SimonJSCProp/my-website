The Six Categories

What We Actually Look At

Most buyers inspect a property for 20 minutes. We spend hours on the data before we ever walk through the door.

🏠

Property Fundamentals

What you're actually buying
  • Full address, listing URL and type (house or townhouse)
  • Construction material and verified build year
  • Beds, baths, parking — confirmed in person
  • Land size (m²) and block slope assessment
  • Building size (m²) and floor plan review
  • Heating and cooling systems itemised
  • Flooring condition and type
  • Building condition rating
  • Street appeal assessment
  • Value-add potential identified
📊

Market & Suburb Data

What the numbers say
  • DSR Score (Demand to Supply Ratio)
  • Auction clearance rate
  • Days on market (DOM)
  • Online search interest (OSI)
  • Median 12-month growth
  • Gross rental yield
  • Vacancy rate (%)
  • Percent stock on market
  • Average vendor discount
  • Typical value (TV) benchmarking
📍

Liveability & Proximity

What attracts tenants and future buyers
  • Distance to nearest train station
  • CBD driving distance
  • Bus routes and major terminals
  • Hospitals and health services
  • Schools, universities and childcare
  • School catchment mapping
  • Shopping centres
  • Open spaces and parks
  • Cafés and restaurant strip access
  • Noise level assessment (road, rail)
💰

Cash Flow Modelling

What lands in your pocket each year
  • Purchase price and LVR modelling
  • Loan repayments (P&I and IO scenarios)
  • Council and water rates
  • Property management fees
  • Building insurance estimate
  • Body corporate or strata fees (if applicable)
  • Estimated maintenance allowance
  • Depreciation estimate (BMT calculator)
  • Current rental income appraisal
  • Positive / Negative cashflow verdict
👥

Demographic Profile

Who lives here and why it matters
  • Owner vs. renter ratio (%)
  • Public and government housing exposure (%)
  • Local employment mix
  • Crime level mapping (QPS data)
  • Population growth and age profile
  • Microburbs liveability score
  • Walk score assessment
  • Proximity to main roads and rail lines
📋

Legal & Compliance

What can go wrong — checked before it does
  • Zoning classification confirmed
  • RPD (Real Property Description)
  • BYDA utility and service overlay check
  • Current tenure and occupancy type
  • Primary and secondary land use
  • Tenancy details — income and lease end date
  • Flood map overlay (all 5 LGA councils)
  • Bushfire risk assessment
  • Koala habitat overlay (QLD Globe)
✅

Every report ends with a clear cashflow verdict.

Cash Flow Positive or Cash Flow Negative — documented, with the full numbers behind it. No guesswork, no vague estimates. You see exactly what the property will cost and return before you make a decision.
